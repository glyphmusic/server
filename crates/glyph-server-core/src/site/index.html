every song, one quiet player.

Glyph searches many legal music sources at once and keeps your library, playlists, lyrics and downloads in one flat, fast app.

download glyph see what it does
free · open source · windows, macos, linux

  

    
search everywhere
Audius, Internet Archive, Deezer, Jamendo and more, merged into one list and ranked by your taste.

    
synced lyrics
Lyrics that follow the song and scroll smoothly as it plays.

    
ascii covers
Optional ASCII-art covers, drawn from real characters like everything else here.

    
sound you control
Crossfade, loudness normalisation, a 10-band equaliser and output device choice.

    
your library, everywhere
An optional @handle account syncs likes, playlists and settings across your devices.

    
share in one link
Send a playlist as a short glyph.my link; friends paste it into Glyph to add it.
